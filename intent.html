Intent of Astrology Arith(m)etic

Astrology Arith(m)etic was created to build a comprehensive foundation of arithmetic and mathematical building blocks for celestial interpretation. The system is deliberately crafted for clarity, accessibility, and repeatable application.

This vault is for the author and trusted continuators who honour the spirit and sovereignty of the work. Although the codex references the Milky Way solar system, its measurements and interpretations extend to other systems and declinations.

The project began in late May and early June of 2025. Milestones and expansions are logged in the Notable Astrology Arith(m)etic Progressions.

Purpose Clarified

Astrology Arith(m)etic is not a book of spells — it is a living archive of energetic mechanics. Celestial bodies and angles are framed as tools and frequencies rather than authorities.

The codex empowers practitioners to design systems, rituals, and analyses grounded in self-defined logic. Interpretations are practical, precise, and sovereign. The work does not seek permission from the sky; it defines how stellar data is harnessed.

I do not ask the sky what it means. I define what I will do with its data.
