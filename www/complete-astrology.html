Complete Astrology Reference Index

This directory reframes and paraphrases insights from Llewellyn’s Complete Book of Astrology: The Easy Way to Learn Astrology by Kris Brandt Riske, M.A. No copyrighted text is reproduced; instead, the author created original summaries, study outlines, and commentary.

Usage Notice: The notes are for private study, non-commercial research, and personal transformative learning. Support the official author by purchasing the book from Llewellyn.com.

Project Purpose

  • Organise essential astrology concepts for structured education.
  • Cross-reference other astrology sources with a consistent taxonomy.
  • Lay the groundwork for training a personal AI assistant tuned to the codex.

Each Markdown file mirrors the structure of the reference text with original notes, insights, and exercises.

Directory Overview

The index follows eight thematic modules:

  1. Introduction — About the book, the author, motivations for study, and how to use the reference.
  2. Basics of Astrology — Historical context, astrological language, chart revelations, and study tips.
  3. Signs — Elemental qualities, complete sign descriptions, and decanate dynamics.
  4. Planets — Planetary functions, dignities, retrograde motion, and sign placements.
  5. Houses — House meanings, individual house dossiers, planetary placements, and house systems.
  6. Aspects — Overview of major and minor aspects with pattern studies and configurations.
  7. Astrology in Action — Applied chart interpretation for relationships, resources, and case studies.
  8. Advanced Topics — Asteroids, fixed stars, lunar nodes, vertex work, and lots.
  9. Resources — Branches of astrology, organisations, recommended reading, and glossaries.

Each subfolder deepens the study path with paraphrased commentary, reflective prompts, and cross-links into the broader Astrology Arith(m)etic vault.
